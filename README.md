# Requirements and UAT Delivery Pack

An independent Business Analyst case study demonstrating how a business need is translated into structured requirements and validated through traceability and user acceptance testing.

## Case Study Scenario

A mid-sized organization currently manages employee service requests through shared email inboxes. Requests are difficult to prioritize, ownership is unclear and employees have limited visibility into progress. The proposed solution is an **Employee Service Request Portal** that centralizes submission, assignment, status tracking, comments and reporting.

## Project Objective

Create a complete requirements-to-UAT delivery pack that provides a clear and traceable path from business objectives through user stories, acceptance criteria, test scenarios and defect management.

## My Role

In this simulated project, I performed the work of a Business Analyst by:

- Defining scope, stakeholders, assumptions and success criteria
- Documenting functional and non-functional requirements
- Writing user stories and testable acceptance criteria
- Defining use cases and business rules
- Building a Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Preparing the UAT strategy, scenarios and exit criteria
- Defining defect severity, workflow and reporting expectations
